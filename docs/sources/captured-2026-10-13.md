# Maker pages read on 2026-10-13

Each section is what a profile in `src/lib/models/profiles/` rests on: the maker's page, the day it was read, and the facts taken from it. These are reading notes (a summary of the page), not a copy. When `npm run models:check` says a source is old, read the page again and update the profile and this file together.

The full Seedance prompt guide is kept as it was read: `byteplus-seedance-2-5-prompt-guide.txt`.

The pages for Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 Omni and Wan 3.0 are drawn by script, so they were read in the built-in browser; the sections below for those three are reading notes taken then.

## Google Veo 3.1: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video

- Length is 4, 6 or 8 s, and must be 8 s with extension, reference images, 1080p or 4K. Extension is 720p only. Veo 3.1 Lite takes no reference images and cannot extend.
- Prompt: subject, action, style, camera, composition, lens, ambiance. Dialogue goes in quotes; sound effects and ambience are stated separately. Audio is always on.

## Kling 3.0 Omni: https://kling.ai/document-api/api/video/3-0-omni/text-to-video

- Several shots are written in the prompt as `shot n, m, words;` — 1 to 6 shots, each at least 1 s, the lengths add up to the clip, 512 characters each. Resolution 720p, 1080p, 4K; length 3 to 15 s. The API returns the actual billing per task.

## Alibaba Wan 3.0: https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/wan3-video-generation-guide (and -api-reference)

- Several shots are a timestamped storyboard in the prompt (`0s - 5s: ...`), 4 to 6 s a shot, up to 30 s. Length 2 to 30 s or -1. Up to 10 reference images, 5 reference videos (1 to 15 s, 15 s together), 5 reference audio clips; a start or end frame cannot be mixed with references.

## BFL FLUX 3 video: https://docs.bfl.ml/api-reference/utility/generate-a-video-with-flux-3.md

- Modes `t2v`, `i2v` (image continuation), `v2v` (video continuation), `draft_enhance`. Unlisted fields are rejected.
- `aspect_ratio` 21:9, 2:1, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, 9:21 or auto. `duration` 5 to 20 s (t2v, i2v) or 5 to 15 s (v2v), or auto. `resolution` hd, fhd, qhd, uhd. `generate_audio` default true. `draft` returns a cache for a later `draft_enhance`.
- `keyframes` 1 to 10 images: one starts the clip, two start and end it, more than two are spread evenly and need a set duration. Timed pairs `[seconds, image]` are also accepted.

## BFL video prompting: https://docs.bfl.ml/guides/prompting_video_overview.md

- Five parts: subject and action, camera direction, scene and atmosphere, motion qualities, continuity constraints.
- Camera examples: static shot, slow push-in, handheld follow, overhead drift, rapid pan. Sound goes after the picture. No special reference tags are documented.

## ElevenLabs audio tags: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/elevenlabs-audio-tags-list

- Tags are natural-language cues in square brackets, comma-separated inside one bracket, placed before the words they change; the feeling carries on until a new tag. Round or curly brackets may be read aloud.
- Works on Eleven v4, v4 Turbo and v3 through the API. Up to 10,000 characters per generation in the Text to Speech app.

## Gemini speech generation: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation

- Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS. Style per turn (`speech_metadata.style`); inline tags in angle brackets such as `<laugh>`, `<sigh>`, `<short pause>`; capitals add emphasis; the text is read verbatim.
- Up to 2 speakers with prebuilt voices; 30 prebuilt voices. No input length limit is stated.

## MiniMax speech: https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/speech-t2a-http

- Models speech-2.8, 2.6, 02, 01 (hd and turbo). Text under 10,000 characters. Pause marker `<#x#>` with x from 0.01 to 99.99 seconds, not twice in a row.
- Interjection tags such as `(laughs)` only on 2.8. Emotion values happy, sad, angry, fearful, disgusted, surprised, calm, fluent, whisper; `fluent` and `whisper` only on 2.6.

## MiniMax Hailuo video: https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-t2v

- Models Hailuo 2.3, 02, T2V-01-Director, T2V-01. 768P allows 6 or 10 s; 1080P is 6 s only. `prompt_optimizer` default true.
- 15 camera commands in square brackets; several in one bracket run together (up to 3 recommended), separate brackets run one after another. Hailuo H3 is not on this page.

## xAI Grok Imagine video: https://docs.x.ai/docs/guides/video-generations

- Image-to-video, text-to-video up to 15 s, reference-to-video (up to 14 images and 3 voice references on 1.5, first, last and mid frames can be pinned), video editing, video extension. The page gives no prompt guidance.
