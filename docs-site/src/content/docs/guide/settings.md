## General

- Save folder: where results are copied.
- Appearance: light or dark, and the light skins.
- Currency: prices are shown in the one you choose.
- Master password: locks the app screen. It is a screen lock, not encryption of your files.

## Backup

Back up to a folder, Google Drive or Dropbox, daily or weekly, and restore from the same place. The backup holds your uploads, results, elements, chats and folders. It never holds your API keys.
