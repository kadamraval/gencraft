## The four parts

- Overview: the cover and the numbers for the story.
- Cast: the characters, locations, props and products, each with a colour, a role, a description and pictures.
- Script: a screenplay page in the standard format. Tab and Return move between scene heading, action, character and dialogue like other screenwriting tools. You can break it into scenes in one step and import or export .fountain files.
- Scene: each beat is a row with its prompt, references, camera, light and four take slots.
- Edit: a timeline to trim, split, reorder, caption and add sound, then export.

## The agent

The Agent on the right works on whichever part you are in: it writes and rewrites the script, or cuts and captions on the timeline. It needs a text model from one of your providers.

## Cutting

- C for the blade, V to select, S to split at the playhead.
- Drag either edge of a clip to trim. Drag a clip to move it.
- Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom the timeline. Space plays.
