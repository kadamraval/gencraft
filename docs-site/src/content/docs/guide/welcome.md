## What it is

Gencraft is a desktop studio for making images, video, voice and finished stories with AI. It does not run models itself. You bring your own keys from providers such as fal, Replicate, OpenRouter, Gemini and OpenAI, and the app sends your prompt to them.

What you make is saved on your device. The providers bill you directly for what you run, and the app shows an estimate of the cost as you go.

## The first five minutes

1. Open Settings, then Providers, and paste one API key.
2. Open Settings, then General, and choose where results are saved.
3. Go to Create, pick a model and write a prompt.
4. Optional: make a brand kit and a few elements, then start a story.

> Tip: The Get started list on Home ticks each of these off for you.
