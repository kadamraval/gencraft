## Two kinds

- Character, location, product and prop are pictures. They are sent to the model as references.
- Camera, light, style and effects are words added to your prompt. Write them, or give an example picture and let a vision model describe it.

## Using them

In Create, open the plus menu, choose Element and pick one. The model's limit on references is shown, and earlier categories keep their slot first when there are too many.
