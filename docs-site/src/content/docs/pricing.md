---
title: "Free and Paid"
description: "What Gencraft includes for free and what Paid adds, with prices."
---

Gencraft is open source and free to use. Paid adds the Agents, Connectors, building your own Apps and email support, and removes the one-project limit. Paid is one plan with two ways to pay: **One Year, $39** or **Lifetime, $99**. The features are the same either way.

You pay the model providers (fal, Replicate, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini) directly in both plans.
