# Trademark policy

The source code of Gencraft is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "Gencraft"** and the **Gencraft logo** are not part of that licence: they identify the official app and its owner.

## What you may do
- Say that your project is *based on* or *compatible with* Gencraft.
- Name a fork honestly, for example "Acme Studio, a fork of Gencraft".
- Refer to Gencraft in articles, videos, reviews and comparisons.

## What you may not do
- Call your fork, build or product "Gencraft", or use a name or logo that could be mistaken for it.
- Present a build you made as the official Gencraft app, its installers or its updates.
- Use the Gencraft name or logo to sell, advertise or support a paid product or service that is not the owner's.

## Why
People who download "Gencraft" should get the app the owner publishes, signed with the owner's keys. The code stays free for everyone; the name tells users whose build they are running.

A fork that wants its own updates uses its own name, its own signing keys and its own update address.
